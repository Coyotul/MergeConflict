# Project Context

## Challenge: AI for Operational Efficiency (Raiffeisen Bank @ VibeHack)

### The Challenge
Build an AI agent that handles repetitive internal banking work involving documents, data, and predefined rules.

### What to Demonstrate
A working process that takes an input, applies the relevant rules, and produces an outcome or flags an exception.

### Data
Use synthetic documents and datasets. Internal banking data is not required.

### Possible Directions
- Reconcile records and identify discrepancies
- Validate applications against requirements
- Triage and summarise requests
- Detect anomalies
