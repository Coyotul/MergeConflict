# Project Context

## Challenge: AI for Operational Efficiency (Raiffeisen Bank @ VibeHack)

### The Challenge
Build an AI agent that handles repetitive internal banking work involving documents, data, and predefined rules.

### What to Demonstrate
A bank-agnostic AI assistant supporting the credit lifecycle: loan applications,
refinancing, early repayment, servicing, contract explanations and financial
scenarios. The AI coordinates; deterministic services calculate and validate;
authorized systems execute; human representatives review consequential decisions.

The MVP demonstrates a synthetic personal-loan journey with cited answers,
preliminary screening, simulations, explicit submission confirmation, a request
for more information, and an authorized human's final decision. It also includes
at least one refinancing or early repayment simulation. No real financial
operations occur.

### Data
Use synthetic documents and datasets. Internal banking data is not required.

### Selected Direction
Use `SPECIFICATION.md` as the main product reference and `ARCHITECTURE.md` for
component ownership. `IDEAS.md` summarizes the selected scope. Raiffeisen Romania
may serve as a public documentation reference; the product must not impersonate
the bank or claim affiliation.
