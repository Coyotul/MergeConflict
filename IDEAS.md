# Project Ideas

Brainstorming notes for the challenge:

## 1. Reconcile Records & Identify Discrepancies
- Ingest bank statements / transaction logs vs accounting ledger.
- Highlight missing, duplicate, or mismatched amounts and fees.

## 2. Validate Applications Against Requirements
- Loan / account opening applications with attached synthetic documents (ID, payslip, statement).
- Verify eligibility against rules (DTI ratio, age, document validity).
- Output: Approved, Rejected, or Flagged for Human Review.

## 3. Triage & Summarise Requests
- Ingest customer or internal requests/tickets.
- Classify priority, route to appropriate department, extract key entities.

## 4. Detect Anomalies
- Monitor synthetic transaction streams for abnormal behavior or compliance/AML rule breaches.
